The thought behind it

Giving deserves
to be seen.

Public Relief is a charity website prototype built around one simple idea: make donation records easier for everyone to explore.

Our point of view

Trust starts with openness.

Donating is an act of hope. This project explores how a public, hash-linked record can show when a donation was added and how its record connects to the one before it.

It is a learning project, not an operating charity. The ledger is a local demonstration, and the site does not collect or transfer real money.

How the demo works

A simple trail to follow.

01

Choose a focus

Explore the sample relief projects and choose where your demonstration donation record should be tagged.

02

Add a record

The form sends your details to a local API, which stores a new record in a JSON file.

03

Follow the ledger

Browse the records and their hashes. Each entry links to the previous record in this prototype chain.

This demo does not verify charitable partners, publish impact reports, process payments, or distribute donations.
